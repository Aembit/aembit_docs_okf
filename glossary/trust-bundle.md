# trust bundle

A collection of Certificate Authority (CA) certificates that a SPIFFE-aware workload uses to validate X.509-SVIDs and other SPIFFE identities presented during authentication.

Learn more: [trust bundle](https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/spiffe-about/spiffe-concepts/#trust-bundle)
