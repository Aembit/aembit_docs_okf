# SVID

SPIFFE Verifiable Identity Document. A cryptographically verifiable credential that proves a workload's identity within a SPIFFE trust domain. Aembit issues SVIDS with short lifetimes and automatic rotation. SVIDs come in two formats — X.509-SVID (certificate) and JWT-SVID (token).

Learn more: [SVID](https://spiffe.io/docs/latest/spiffe-about/spiffe-concepts/#spiffe-verifiable-identity-document-svid)
