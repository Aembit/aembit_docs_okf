# SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2)

An auditing standard for service providers that store customer data in the cloud. SOC 2 Type II reports demonstrate the effectiveness of security controls over time across five Trust Service Criteria - Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy.

Learn more: [SOC 2 (Service Organization Control 2)](https://www.aicpa.org/soc2)
