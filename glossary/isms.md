# ISMS (Information Security Management System)

A systematic approach to managing sensitive information, ensuring confidentiality, integrity, and availability. ISO 27001 certifies that an organization's ISMS meets international standards for risk management and security controls.

Learn more: [ISMS (Information Security Management System)](https://www.iso.org/isoiec-27001-information-security.html)
